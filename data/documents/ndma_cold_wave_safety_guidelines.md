# NDMA Guidelines: Prevention and Management of Cold Wave and Frost

**Source Authority:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Government of India  
**Official Portal:** https://ndma.gov.in/Natural-Hazards/Cold-Wave  
**Category:** cold_wave  
**Document Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Definition and Criteria for Cold Wave in India
The India Meteorological Department (IMD) defines a cold wave for the plains based on minimum temperatures and departure from normal:

- **Core Threshold:** The minimum temperature of the station is 10°C or less (for plains).
- **Cold Wave:** 
  - Departure from normal minimum temperature is between -4.5°C and -6.4°C.
  - Or, the actual minimum temperature is 4.0°C or lower.
- **Severe Cold Wave:** 
  - Departure from normal minimum temperature is greater than -6.4°C.
  - Or, the actual minimum temperature is 2.0°C or lower.

For coastal stations, a cold wave is declared when the minimum temperature is 15°C or lower and the departure from normal is -4.5°C or less.

---

## 2. Health Risks: Hypothermia and Frostbite
- **Hypothermia:** A medical emergency where the body loses heat faster than it can produce it, causing core body temperature to drop below 35°C (95°F). Symptoms include uncontrollable shivering, slow/slurred speech, clumsy fingers, apathy, confusion, and extreme fatigue.
- **Frostbite:** Freezing of skin and tissue, usually affecting fingers, toes, nose, and ears. Skin turns pale, hard, cold, and numb.

## 3. Protective Actions and Dos & Don'ts

### Dos:
- Dress in multiple layers of loose-fitting, lightweight, warm clothing rather than a single heavy layer (air trapped between layers acts as an insulator).
- Protect your head, neck, hands, and feet: wear woolen caps/beanies, scarves, mittens, and insulated waterproof shoes (most body heat is lost through the head).
- Eat regular, warm, calorie-dense nutritious food and drink warm fluids (hot tea, soups, warm milk) to fuel body metabolism.
- Keep elderly people and young children indoors in heated, draft-free rooms.

### Don'ts:
- Do NOT consume alcohol to "warm up." Alcohol dilates surface blood vessels, giving a false sensation of warmth while rapidly stripping internal core body heat.
- Do NOT use unvented gas heaters, charcoal fires (angithi), or kerosene stoves in closed, unventilated rooms. Burning charcoal in closed rooms produces odorless, lethal **carbon monoxide (CO)** gas that causes asphyxiation and death.
- Avoid prolonged exposure to cold winds and damp clothing.
