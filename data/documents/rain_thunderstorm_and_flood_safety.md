# Urban Rain, Thunderstorm, and Monsoon Flood Safety Guidelines

**Source Authority:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) & IMD  
**Official Portal:** https://ndma.gov.in/Natural-Hazards/Floods  
**Category:** rain_flood  
**Document Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Daily Rain Advisories & Practical Decisions

### When to Carry an Umbrella or Raincoat:
- If precipitation probability is **30% or higher**, or the weather forecast indicates light showers, intermittent drizzle, or overcast skies with high humidity, carrying a compact umbrella or rain jacket is strongly advised.
- Carrying protective rain gear keeps clothing dry, prevents sudden hypothermic cooling or seasonal respiratory infections, and protects electronics (laptops, phones).

### Rainfall Classifications by IMD:
- **Very Light Rain:** 0.1 mm to 2.4 mm in 24 hours.
- **Light Rain:** 2.5 mm to 15.5 mm in 24 hours.
- **Moderate Rain:** 15.6 mm to 64.4 mm in 24 hours.
- **Heavy Rain:** 64.5 mm to 115.5 mm in 24 hours (Yellow to Orange alert).
- **Very Heavy Rain:** 115.6 mm to 204.4 mm in 24 hours (Orange to Red alert).
- **Extremely Heavy Rain:** Greater than 204.4 mm in 24 hours (Red alert).

## 2. Lightning and Thunderstorm Safety Rules
Lightning kills thousands worldwide each year. Follow the universal **30-30 Rule**:
- If the time between seeing lightning and hearing thunder is less than 30 seconds, seek shelter immediately.
- Remain in shelter for at least 30 minutes after hearing the last clap of thunder.

### Outdoor Lightning Don'ts:
- Do NOT seek shelter under isolated trees, open sheds, picnic shelters, or metal towers.
- Stay away from bodies of water (lakes, swimming pools, rivers) and metal objects (fences, golf clubs, bicycles).
- If caught in an open field with no shelter, crouch down low on the balls of your feet with hands on your knees and head tucked. Do NOT lie flat on the ground.
- The safest shelters are enclosed buildings with plumbing/wiring or hardtop enclosed vehicles.

## 3. Flash Floods and Urban Waterlogging Safety
- **"Turn Around, Don't Drown":** Never attempt to walk, swim, or drive through moving water or flooded underpasses.
- Just **6 inches (15 cm)** of rapidly moving water can knock an adult off their feet.
- Just **12 inches (30 cm)** of moving water can float or carry away small passenger cars.
- Avoid stepping near submerged electrical junction boxes, downed wires, or open drains/manholes hidden by muddy water.
