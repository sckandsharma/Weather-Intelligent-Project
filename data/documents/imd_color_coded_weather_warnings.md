# IMD Standard Operating Procedure: Color-Coded Weather Warnings

**Source Authority:** India Meteorological Department (IMD), Ministry of Earth Sciences  
**Official Portal:** https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/warning_guidelines.php  
**Category:** weather_alerts  
**Document Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Overview of IMD Warning System
The India Meteorological Department (IMD) issues universal color-coded weather warnings to alert disaster management authorities and the general public regarding anticipated hazardous weather events such as intense rainfall, heat waves, cold waves, thunderstorms, cyclones, and blizzards.

The system uses four standardized colors: **Green, Yellow, Orange, and Red**, reflecting severity, impact level, and the urgency of required response.

---

## 2. Meaning of IMD Alert Colors

### Green Alert (No Warning)
- **Meaning:** "All is well / No advisory."
- **Expected Weather:** Normal or non-hazardous atmospheric conditions.
- **Impact:** No danger to daily life, travel, or infrastructure.
- **Required Public Action:** None. Routine activities can proceed without disruption.

### Yellow Alert (Watch / Be Updated)
- **Meaning:** "Watch and stay updated."
- **Expected Weather:** Mild to moderately severe weather that could worsen over the next few days.
- **Impact:** Minor disruptions to local commute, intermittent traffic slowdowns, or localized water ponding.
- **Required Public Action:** Keep track of updated weather bulletins and local news before undertaking long outdoor travels.

### Orange Alert (Alert / Be Prepared)
- **Meaning:** "Be prepared / High risk of dangerous weather."
- **Expected Weather:** Very heavy rainfall, severe heatwave, cyclonic winds, or destructive thunderstorms.
- **Impact:** High likelihood of significant disruption. Power outages, structural damage to thatched houses, uprooting of small trees, disruption of road, rail, and air traffic. Risk of injury or threat to life if outdoors.
- **Required Public Action:** Stay prepared. Stock essential medicines, potable water, and emergency food supplies. Avoid unnecessary travel. Outdoor laborers should suspend operations during peak alert hours. Secure loose objects outdoors.

### Red Alert (Warning / Take Action)
- **Meaning:** "Take immediate action / Extreme danger."
- **Expected Weather:** Extremely severe weather event occurring or imminent within 24 hours. Includes torrential rainfall (>204.4 mm), severe cyclonic storms, or extreme heat emergencies.
- **Impact:** High confidence of major disaster, catastrophic infrastructure damage, loss of life, widespread flooding, complete collapse of transportation and power networks.
- **Required Public Action:** Mandatory safety precautions. Stay indoors in secure, reinforced buildings. Evacuate low-lying areas if directed by disaster management authorities. Follow official directives strictly and do not venture outside under any circumstances.
