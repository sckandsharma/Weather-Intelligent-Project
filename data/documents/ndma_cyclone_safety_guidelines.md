# NDMA Guidelines: Cyclone Preparedness and Safety Measures

**Source Authority:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Government of India  
**Official Portal:** https://ndma.gov.in/Natural-Hazards/Cyclone  
**Category:** cyclone  
**Document Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. What is a Tropical Cyclone?
A tropical cyclone is an intense atmospheric low-pressure weather system originating over warm tropical oceans. It features spiraling winds rotating inward toward a central low-pressure calm area known as the **"eye"** of the cyclone. Cyclones are characterized by violent gale-force winds, torrential rain, and devastating coastal storm surges.

---

## 2. Cyclone Warning Stages in India
IMD operates a 4-stage cyclone warning mechanism:
1. **Pre-Cyclone Watch (72 hours prior):** Issued when a cyclonic disturbance shows potential for coastal development.
2. **Cyclone Alert - Yellow (48 hours prior):** Issued to state and district administrations indicating cyclone landfall likelihood.
3. **Cyclone Warning - Orange (24 hours prior):** Landfall area and expected wind speeds specified; coastal evacuation commences.
4. **Post-Landfall Outlook - Red (12 hours prior to landfall):** High emergency alert for storm surge, destructive inland winds, and flash floods.

---

## 3. Precautions: Before the Cyclone
- Keep an emergency kit ready: battery-operated radio, waterproof flashlights, extra batteries, first-aid kit, dry rations, and bottled drinking water.
- Check and repair the house: secure loose tin roofs, fasten doors and windows, and trim dead branches of trees near the house.
- Anchor outdoor objects (bicycles, signs, tin sheets, water tanks) that could turn into lethal flying projectiles during high winds.
- Keep documents, insurance policies, and valuables sealed in waterproof plastic containers.
- Fishermen must not venture into the sea; boats and trawlers should be hauled to high ground and safely anchored.

---

## 4. Precautions: During the Cyclone
- Stay indoors inside the strongest, safest part of the house, away from windows and glass panes.
- Switch off the electrical main supply and turn off gas cylinders to prevent short circuits and fire hazards.
- Drink only boiled or chlorination-treated water to avoid waterborne contaminants.
- Listen only to official bulletins via radio or mobile alerts; do not believe or spread unverified rumors.
- **The "Eye" Warning:** If the strong wind suddenly stops and skies clear, DO NOT venture outside. This is the eye of the cyclone passing overhead; the wind will abruptly restart from the opposite direction with equal or greater fury within minutes.

---

## 5. Precautions: After the Cyclone
- Remain inside until official authorities declare that the storm has fully passed.
- Beware of dangling electric wires, broken power poles, gas leaks, and weakened structures.
- Do not drive through flooded roads or cross damaged bridges.
- Clean and disinfect contaminated premises before cooking or resting.
